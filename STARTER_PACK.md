# Starter pack

Sounds in `app/src/main/assets/starter/` are copied into every new install and show up in the Sounds tab
(and the floating board). They don't use up free-plan slots.

To add your own: drop audio files into that folder, then run `node tools/starter-index.js`
to refresh `index.json`. Only ship sounds you made yourself or have the rights to (CC0, or CC-BY with credit).
The 13 sounds that come with the app were synthesized for FloatBoard, so there's nothing to credit.
